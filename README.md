# 40 Ways to Make Money with GPT Image 2.6's GIF and SVG Features

English | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)

GPT Image 2.6, which OpenAI released in November 2026, can output animated GIFs (2–16 frames) and SVG vector images directly. This list collects 40 ways these two features could earn money, grouped into six categories. Each entry covers four things: how to make it, where to sell it, what it pays, and the rules you have to follow. Generation settings, post-processing steps and a prompt template are collected at the end.

**Compiled by:** the [apimodels.app](https://apimodels.app) team (we provide GPT Image 2.6 and many other models through one API key). Sources are public platform rules and public reporting, current as of 2026-11-07. Platform rules change, so check the platform's latest terms before you list anything. This is not a promise of income.

## I. Sticker and decoration platforms in China

### 1 WeChat stickers with tipping
- How: Generate a separate 6–8 frame GIF for each sticker from a reference image, remove the background, resize to 240×240, add a 2 px white outline, and keep each file ≤500 KB. A set has 8–24 stickers ([platform guide](https://sticker.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmemoticon-bin/readtemplate?t=guide/index) (Chinese)).
- Where to sell: On desktop, list on the WeChat Sticker Store at sticker.weixin.qq.com. Registering as an individual requires a mainland China ID card and a WeChat account linked to a bank card. Creators on WeChat Channels (WeChat's short-video feed) can also list from their phone through the "微信表情助手" (WeChat Sticker Assistant) mini program ([Guangming Online](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-04/14/content_1304417203.htm) (Chinese)).
- Price: Users choose how much to tip, and currently the full amount goes to the creator's WeChat Pay balance ([platform guide](https://sticker.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmemoticon-bin/readtemplate?t=guide/index) (Chinese)).
- Rules: Work must be original; derivative works and fan art are not accepted. If the upload form has an AI disclosure option, tick it truthfully ([AI content labeling measures](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm) (Chinese)).

### 2 Paid WeChat stickers
- How: Production is the same as item 1. To qualify, you need one set already live and no violations in the past three months; then apply for the paid feature in the creator dashboard.
- Price: A flat 10 WeChat Beans (微信豆, WeChat's virtual currency) per set, settled monthly ([platform guide](https://sticker.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmemoticon-bin/readtemplate?t=guide/index) (Chinese)).
- Rules: Paid stickers are exclusive, so you cannot submit them to any other platform, and once a set is paid you cannot switch it back to free.

### 3 Original character IP
- How: Commit to one original character, keep releasing new sticker sets, and build a "sticker character" (表情形象) collection page on WeChat. Also make an SVG vector master of the character for merch printing.
- Where to sell: Character licensing, Taobao merch (RMB 10–200 per item) and brand deals ([36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/883703833707521) (Chinese)).
- Example: Mogutou (蘑菇头, the mushroom-head meme character) earns mainly from IP licensing; its rights holder once claimed RMB 49,479 from a company over 7 stickers ([The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_26087900) (Chinese)).
- Rules: Keep evidence of your human creative work and register the work for copyright. Do not describe it as "XX-style".

### 4 QQ themes and decorations
- How: Use GIFs to make animated pendants and chat bubbles for QQ (Tencent's messaging app), cut out as transparent PNG frame sequences.
- Where to sell: On the Tencent Design Open Platform, click "变现" (Monetize), go to "美化平台" (the personalization platform) and apply; you sign a contract after approval ([Tencent Design Open Platform](https://topd.tencent.com/realize) (Chinese), [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/question/422381694) (Chinese)).
- Rules: The revenue share, size specs and AI policy are not public. Go by what you are told when you sign.

### 5–6 WeChat red-envelope covers: official paid covers and design for clients
- How: Red-envelope covers are custom skins for the red packets people send on WeChat. Make a small looping animation of 8 frames × 200 ms and convert it to a 3:4 MP4 of 1–3 seconds or a PNG frame sequence. Pendants and bubbles need transparent backgrounds ([Tencent Cloud Developer Community](https://cloud.tencent.cn/developer/article/2488056) (Chinese)).
- Where to sell: To join, your WeChat Official Account or Channels account needs 100 followers. To unlock paid covers, you also need to have issued at least 1 cover in the past year ([Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20240810A00DPY00) (Chinese), [Chinaz](https://www.chinaz.com/feed/0125/1666228.shtml) (Chinese)).
- Price: You set your own price for paid covers. Once a single cover's revenue reaches 50 heat points (热度值), the platform takes 20%; below that, it charges RMB 1 per order. For design work, the client pays the issuing fee of RMB 1 per cover ([Chinaz](https://www.chinaz.com/feed/0125/1666228.shtml) (Chinese), [Guizang](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250108A0807J00) (Chinese)).
- Rules: Covers and their serial codes may not be sold or transferred; violations get the covers revoked and taken down ([WeChat Help Center](https://support.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmsupportacctnodeweb-bin/pages/Z7N8dcm17bQgRN33) (Chinese)). Chinese New Year 2027 falls around February 6, and your first cover has to be issued before December.

## II. Sticker and emoji platforms outside China

### 7 LINE
- How: Pick 6–8 distinct frames and convert them to transparent APNG, no larger than 320×270, ≤4 seconds, ≤1 MB per file ([LINE guidelines](https://creator.line.me/en/guideline/animationsticker/)).
- Where to sell: List through the web version of Creators Market. Payouts only go to a PayPal Premier or Business account, and you need at least 1,000 yen to withdraw. Residents of mainland China should first confirm that their PayPal business account will work ([LINE Help](https://help2.line.me/creators/web/categoryId/20002319/3/pc?lang=en)).
- Price: Creator share = (sale price − platform fee) × 50%. Animated stickers are priced in tiers from 240 to 600 yen. The subscription service, Premium, pays creators by "subscribers × 12 yen × share of sends" ([LINE Help](https://help2.line.me/creators/web/categoryId/20002319/3/pc?lang=en), [LINE 2016](https://linepluscorp.com/pr/news/en/2016/1391)).
- Rules: Set "Use of AI" when you submit, and do not write "XXXX-style" in titles or descriptions ([LINE Help](https://help2.line.me/creators/ios/pc?contentId=20005099&lang=en)).

### 8 Telegram
- How: Convert to WEBM VP9, exactly 512 px on one side, ≤3 seconds, ≤256 KB ([Telegram](https://core.telegram.org/stickers)). sticker-convert can upload directly ([GitHub](https://github.com/laggykiller/sticker-convert)).
- Where to sell: Telegram does not let you put a price on a sticker pack. The money comes from custom work (items 16–17) or a bot that charges in Stars (item 23).

### 9 iMessage
- How: Make 408×408 APNGs, ≤500 KB each. One pack cannot mix sizes ([Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/imessage-apps-and-stickers)).
- Where to sell: Publish on the App Store; a developer account costs $99 a year. Under the Small Business Program, the platform commission is 15% ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/)).

### 10 WhatsApp
- How: Make 512×512 animated WebPs, ≤500 KB each. The first frame must show the full image, and each pack has 3–30 stickers ([WhatsApp](https://github.com/WhatsApp/stickers/blob/main/Android/README.md)).
- Where to sell: WhatsApp itself does not sell stickers, so you build a third-party sticker app that earns from ads and in-app purchases. A third-party estimate puts the top sticker-maker apps at $10,000 to $50,000+ a month ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/stickerwhatsapp.com.stickers/trends)).
- Rules: If the app has AI generation features, it must include a way to report content ([Google Play](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2024/06/enabling-safe-ai-experiences.html?hl=en)).

### 11 Kakao
- How: Use AI only for planning and captions; the final art must be redrawn by hand. Animated emoticons are 360×360, and GIFs can have at most 24 frames ([ice-ice-bear](https://ice-ice-bear.github.io/posts/2026-04-22-chatgpt-kakao-emoji-viability/), [Soomgo](https://soomgo.com/a.%EC%B6%A9%EB%B6%81-%EA%B4%B4%EC%82%B0%EA%B5%B0/%EC%BA%90%EB%A6%AD%ED%84%B0-%EC%9D%B4%EB%AA%A8%ED%8B%B0%EC%BD%98-%EC%A0%9C%EC%9E%91) (Korean)).
- Price: A set costs 2,500 won, and the creator gets about 35–40% (from secondary sources).
- Rules: Kakao restricts generative-AI emoticons from its store ([SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000135095) (Korean)). There is no public information on whether or how foreign creators can register.

### 12 GIPHY / Klipy
- How: Make reaction GIFs with an original character. Once you have uploaded 5, apply for a Creator Channel and put your LINE or Fiverr link in your profile bio ([GIPHY](https://support.giphy.com/hc/en-us/articles/4631422637082-Apply-for-a-Creator-Channel)).
- Where to sell: Neither platform pays per view. After the Tenor API shut down on 2026-06-30, WhatsApp's GIF search switched to GIPHY ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/30/google-tenor-api-gif-updates/)). To work with Klipy, submit a proposal through the GRIN platform ([GRIN](https://app.grin.ai/brands/klipy.com)).

## III. Custom services

### 13 Custom stickers for buyers in China
- How: Ask the client for 3–5 front-facing photos, make one character design for them to approve, then make the GIFs one by one. Offer two delivery tiers: one for saving to WeChat Favorites, one for listing on the Sticker Store.
- Where to find clients: Xianyu (Alibaba's second-hand marketplace) and Xiaohongshu (RedNote). Xiaohongshu bans moving customers off the platform to pay, so deals have to go through the Xiaohongshu store ([Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250312A05A1Y00) (Chinese)).
- Price: RMB 9.9–99 per set ([Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2047334032057414870) (Chinese)). For comparison, a single hand-drawn sticker costs RMB 200–300 ([Lancheng Visual](http://www.cdwjyx.cn/23news/14378.html) (Chinese)).
- Rules: Only accept photos of the client or their family, and get written consent. Do not take orders for celebrities or existing IP. Custom goods are exempt from China's seven-day no-reason return rule, but the listing must say so clearly ([The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32772328) (Chinese)).

### 14–15 Sticker packs for companies, brands, stores and government
- How: If the client already has a mascot, use it as the reference image and make 16 animated stickers in one batch. If not, design the character first and make an SVG master.
- Where to find clients: Design agencies, Zhubajie (a Chinese freelance marketplace) and direct contacts at companies. You can help clients publish these as free promotional stickers; they do not appear in the Sticker Store but can be found through search ([platform guide](https://sticker.weixin.qq.com/cgi-bin/mmemoticon-bin/readtemplate?t=guide/index) (Chinese)).
- Price: 16 animated stickers for RMB 4,800–9,600 ([Lancheng Visual](http://www.cdwjyx.cn/23news/14378.html) (Chinese), 2023); a mascot plus sticker pack for RMB 7,000–10,000 ([Zhubajie](https://www.zbj.com/fuwu/dtbqsj/) (Chinese)); Xuwei New Area's Chinese New Year sticker pack had a contract price of RMB 68,000 ([Xuwei New Area](http://ggzy.xwxq.gov.cn/home/tradeDetail?type=1&pid=3545&nid=4892) (Chinese), 2024).
- Rules: Sign a contract, issue invoices and include a copyright transfer clause. For government projects, bid as a registered company or work as a subcontractor to the winning bidder.

### 16 Custom stickers for buyers outside China
- How: Deliver the same character in a version for each platform: WebP (WhatsApp), WEBM (Telegram), APNG (iMessage) and transparent PNG.
- Where to find clients: Fiverr, Etsy, Instagram DMs.
- Price: A sticker pack on Fiverr is $25–65 ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/nomueraspolilla/design-a-telegram-sticker-pack-for-you)); an Etsy digital pet portrait is $32.50 ([Etsy](https://etsy.com/listing/1274715432/cartoon-pet-portrait-custom-pet-portrait)); Instagram commissions run $50 for 8, $90 for 16 and $120 for 24 ([Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-04-17/doc-inhuuyih0984239.shtml) (Chinese)).
- Rules: On Fiverr, you must disclose AI use when the client asks, and if the client asks you not to use AI, you must comply ([Fiverr Help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593-Using-AI-on-Fiverr-Guidelines-for-freelancers-and-clients)). On Etsy, state AI use in the listing description.

### 17 Community and company emoji
- Price: Telegram sticker packs for crypto projects sell for $25–50, and this service has 597 reviews ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/myselfrobiul/create-telegram-stickers-pack-for-your-crypto-project)). Emoji packs of 10–20 sell for $5–15 each ([MakeEmoji](https://makeemoji.com/blog/monetizing-custom-emojis-pricing-guide)).
- How: Slack emoji are 128×128 and about 128 KB; Discord emoji are ≤256 KB ([CommunityOne](https://blog.communityone.io/discord-gif-emoji-size/)).

### 18 Streamer emotes
- How: Use the streamer's avatar or character art as the reference image, make a 6–8 frame animation, export at 112, 56 and 28 px, and keep flashing to no more than 3 times per second ([Pixotter](https://pixotter.pages.dev/blog/twitch-emote-size/)).
- Where to find clients: Fiverr has 1,900+ similar gigs, starting at $15–41 ([Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/search/gigs?query=animated%20twitch%20emotes)).
- Price: Human artists charge $25 for 1 and $65 for 3 ([laziyu](https://laziyu-emotes.carrd.co/)).
- Rules: VGen and Skeb do not accept AI work ([VGen](https://help.vgen.co/hc/en-us/articles/12882944163991), [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2022-12-27/japanese-art-commission-platform-skeb-bans-ai-art/.193383)). Take AI orders on Fiverr or your own page, and label them "AI-assisted, hand-finished".

- Price: VectoSolve charges $7.99 a month or $49 a year, with Stripe-verified revenue of $3,110 over the last 30 days ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vectosolve)); SVG Bloom sells 100 credits for $9.99 ([G2](https://ai.g2.com/marketplace/tools/svg-bloom)).

### 23 Telegram / Discord bots

### 24 Apps for global markets

- Rules: The OpenAI API does not support mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau, so only list the app in supported regions ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/supported-countries)).

### 25 Mini programs and websites in China
- Requirements: WeChat mini programs (apps that run inside WeChat) registered to an individual cannot get the deep synthesis category (China's regulatory term for generative AI), so you need a company entity ([WeChat docs](https://developers.weixin.qq.com/minigame/dev/wxcloud/guide/extensions/extend/ai.md) (Chinese)). The entity that files the algorithm registration must match the contracting entity ([CSDN](https://adg.csdn.net/6a699332662f9a54cb95a377.html) (Chinese)). Images that users download or export must carry an AI label ([Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20250319/a572ec4366f149c1b0b04a07ebf4931b/c.html) (Chinese)).
- Approach: For the China version, use a domestic model that has completed registration. Build the GPT Image 2.6 version as a product for markets outside China.
- Price: One AI red-envelope cover generator site charges RMB 9.9 for 10 uses ([hongbao.elvinn.wiki](https://hongbao.elvinn.wiki/pricing) (Chinese)).

## V. SVG work

### 26 Logo design service
- How: Start with 10–20 SVG concept directions (about $0.63 in generation fees), revise them by hand, then deliver a full set of SVG, AI, PDF and PNG files.
- Price: Zhubajie's reference price is RMB 800–6,000 ([Zhubajie](https://tf.zbj.com/sem/pc/yxlogo?pmcode=128273572) (Chinese)); common Fiverr prices are $30–50 ([WebsiteBuilderInsider](https://www.websitebuilderinsider.com/how-much-does-a-logo-cost-on-fiverr/)).
- Rules: Run a trademark search before delivery, and spell out the scope of the copyright transfer in the contract ([U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html)).

### 27 Raster-to-vector service
- How: For simple logos, have GPT Image 2.6 redraw them as SVG from a text description. For complex images, use a tracing tool.
- Price: On Etsy, "AI logo to vector" services are $15–24.39 and photo-to-SVG is $5 ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/search?q=ai+generated+svg+cricut)); on Dribbble, from $59 ([Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/services/83603-Convert-to-Vector-Image-Tracing-Service)).

### 28 Laser / vinyl-cutting / embroidery files
- How: Make engraving designs such as badges and silhouettes, with cut lines as closed single-line paths. Embroidery needs a separate conversion to PES or DST ([Creative Fabrica](https://help.creativefabrica.com/hc/en-us/articles/25796577543196-What-are-the-guidelines-for-products-on-Creative-Fabrica)).
- Where to sell: Etsy, Creative Fabrica, Glowforge Catalog. Glowforge Premium members can become Catalog designers for free, and the platform takes no commission ([Glowforge](https://support.glowforge.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260804788069-glowforge-catalog-designer-faq)).
- Price: Laser kits on Etsy sell for $3.49–14.99 ([Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/search?q=svg+cut+file)).

### 29 Atomm community
- Price: Creators get 80% on paid projects and up to 30% commission on materials, and can withdraw once they reach $20. The platform says its top creators earn $3,000+ a month ([Atomm](https://www.atomm.com/creatorprogram)).
- How: Test the design on an xTool laser first, then publish the finished project together with the materials and settings.

### 30 Stock site submissions

- Rules: Adobe requires you to tick the AI label, and you cannot submit multiple versions from the same prompt ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html)). On Qianku (千库网, another Chinese stock site), the copyright of approved work belongs to the platform. Hellorf (站酷海洛, ZCOOL's stock site) only accepts images made with Meitu WHEE and Midjourney ([Hellorf](https://www.hellorf.com/subject/aigc-activity) (Chinese)). ## Shared production workflow

- Settings (tested by apimodels.app on 2026-11-07): frame_count accepts 2–16, and anything above 16 returns a 400 error, so claims online of "2–64 frames" are out of date. Combinations of 4–16 frames at 512–960 px per frame take 10–24 seconds. 4 frames at 512×512 on low quality cost about $0.015 per call. GIFs are not guaranteed to have transparent backgrounds. Passing a reference image keeps the character consistent, and GIF editing with a reference image works normally. SVG supports text-to-image only, 1 image per call, with a maximum canvas of about 1K. The quality parameter has no effect on SVG, and each SVG costs $0.0315.
- Frame count: 6–8 frames give the clearest stickers. 12–16 frames move more smoothly, but each frame is only about 720 px.
- Post-processing: Split frames → remove the background with solid-color keying or rembg → crop all frames with the same bounding box → resize. When exporting GIF, binarize the alpha and add a 2 px white outline; when exporting APNG, WebP or WEBM, keep 8-bit alpha. Finally, package and compress to each platform's spec. sticker-convert can do this in batches ([sticker-convert](https://github.com/laggykiller/sticker-convert)).
- SVG cleanup: Remove the background rectangle, change colors to currentColor and optimize with svgo. For cut files, close the paths and split them into layers ([allsvgicons](https://allsvgicons.com/blog/fixing-ai-generated-svgs-cleaning-paths/)).
- Compliance: Keep your prompts and original files, and declare AI use the way each platform requires.

```text
[Character anchor: shape, colors, outfit; reuse word for word every time] Keep the exact character from the reference image. Do not redesign the character.
The character [one small action], then returns to its starting pose. Fixed camera, same framing in every frame, everything else stays still.
Flat illustration on a plain solid [#FF00FF] background. No text. Seamless loop.
Parameters: output_format=gif, frame_count=6–8, frame_duration_ms=100–150, attach a reference image
```
